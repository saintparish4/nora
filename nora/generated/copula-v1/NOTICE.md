real patient. They were drawn from a statistical model fit to 42
admissions of the MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo, version 2.2:

> Johnson, A., Bulgarelli, L., Pollard, T., Horng, S., Celi, L. A., and
> Mark, R. MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo (version 2.2). PhysioNet.

# Quick Booking - User Guide

## Book Your Appointment in Under 2 Minutes! ⚡

### What is Quick Booking?

Quick Booking is NORA's fastest way to get healthcare. Simply describe what you're experiencing, and our AI will instantly match you with the right provider and help you book an appointment - all in one smooth flow.

---

## How to Use Quick Booking

### Step 1: Start Quick Booking

**From the Landing Page:**
- Click the blue "⚡ Book in Under 2 Minutes" button

**From the Dashboard:**
- Click "⚡ Quick Booking" in the left sidebar
- Or select "Book in 2 Minutes" from the menu

### Step 2: Describe Your Symptoms (30 seconds)

Tell us what you're experiencing. Be as detailed as possible:

**Good Examples:**
- ✅ "I've had a persistent headache for 3 days, feel dizzy, and I'm sensitive to light"
- ✅ "Sharp pain in my lower back that started after lifting heavy boxes yesterday"
- ✅ "Feeling very anxious and having trouble sleeping for the past week"

**Less Helpful:**
- ❌ "I feel sick"
- ❌ "Pain"

**Tips:**
- Minimum 10 characters required
- Include when symptoms started
- Mention severity and any triggers
- Note any related symptoms

Click **"Analyze & Find Providers"** when ready.

### Step 3: Review AI Analysis (15 seconds)

Our AI will:
- ✅ Recommend the right type of specialist
- ✅ Assess urgency level
- ✅ Identify key symptoms
- ✅ Show you matching providers

**Urgency Levels:**
- 🟢 **Routine**: Can be scheduled within 1-2 weeks
- 🟠 **Urgent**: Should be seen within 24-48 hours
- 🔴 **Emergency**: Seek immediate medical attention

### Step 4: Choose a Provider (30 seconds)

You'll see up to 5 providers who match your needs. Each card shows:

- 👤 Provider name and photo
- 🏥 Specialty
- ⭐ Rating
- 📍 Location
- 💰 Hourly rate
- 📅 Next available appointment

**Simply click on any provider to continue.**

### Step 5: Select a Time Slot (30 seconds)

You'll see the next 3 available time slots for your chosen provider.

Each slot shows:
- 📅 Date (e.g., "Mon, Nov 18")
- 🕐 Time range (e.g., "9:00 AM - 10:00 AM")

**Click on your preferred time slot.**

**Don't see a good time?**
- Click "Back to Providers" to try another provider
- More time slots will be available in the future

### Step 6: Confirm Your Appointment (15 seconds)

Review your booking details:
- ✅ Provider information
- ✅ Date and time
- ✅ Your symptoms (automatically included as notes)

**Click "Confirm Booking"** to finalize.

### Step 7: You're All Set! 🎉

**Success!** Your appointment is confirmed.

You'll receive:
- ✉️ Email confirmation with all details
- 📱 Reminder 24 hours before (if enabled in settings)

**Next Steps:**
- Click "View Appointments" to see all your bookings
- Click "Book Another Appointment" to schedule more

## Tips for Success

### Get the Best Match
- Be specific about your symptoms
- Mention duration and severity
- Include relevant medical history if applicable

### Choose the Right Time
- Book during times you're likely to be available
- Check your calendar before confirming
- Consider travel time to the location

### Before Your Appointment
- Check your email for confirmation details
- Note the provider's location
- Prepare any questions you want to ask
- Bring relevant medical records if needed

## Common Questions

### Q: How accurate is the AI recommendation?
**A:** Our AI uses advanced medical knowledge to recommend specialists, but it's not a diagnosis. The provider will make the final assessment during your appointment.

### Q: Can I cancel or reschedule?
**A:** Yes! Go to "Appointments" in the sidebar and click on your appointment to cancel or reschedule.

### Q: What if I need a different specialty?
**A:** You can always use "Browse Providers" to see all available specialists, or start a new Quick Booking with different symptoms.

### Q: Is my information secure?
**A:** Yes. All your health information is encrypted and HIPAA-compliant. We never share your data without your permission.

### Q: What if the provider isn't available?
**A:** Try selecting a different provider from the list, or use the regular booking flow to see more availability.

### Q: Can I add notes after booking?
**A:** Currently, notes are added during booking (your symptom description). Contact the provider's office directly to add additional information.

## Urgency Guidelines

### 🟢 Routine (Schedule within 1-2 weeks)
- Annual checkups
- Mild symptoms
- Preventive care
- Follow-up appointments
- Chronic condition management

### 🟠 Urgent (Schedule within 24-48 hours)
- Moderate pain
- Persistent symptoms
- Worsening conditions
- Mental health concerns
- Minor injuries

### 🔴 Emergency (Seek immediate care)
If you experience any of these, **call 911 or go to the nearest ER**:
- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing
- Severe bleeding
- Loss of consciousness
- Stroke symptoms (FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 911)
- Severe allergic reactions
- Suicidal thoughts
